## Summary

- **33 of 39** items: **PASS** — verified working, with evidence.
- **3 of 39** items (28, 29, 30 — EAS builds): **BLOCKED** on a
  user-provisioned EAS project/`EXPO_TOKEN`, which cannot be provisioned by
  an agent. Everything on this repository's side of that boundary (workflow
  files, build profiles, channel mapping) is built and validated as far as
  it can be without those credentials.
- **2 of 39** items (31 environment strategy, 32 CI): **PARTIAL** — real,
  working, but incomplete against the full original intent, with the gap
  documented rather than hidden.
- **1 of 39** items (39, "new app without modifying shared infra"):
  **PARTIAL** — designed and consistently followed, but never empirically
  proven with a real second app.
- **1 of 39** items (35, accessibility): **NOT DONE** — the one item this
  audit exists specifically to be capable of catching. No prior phase
  claimed it was done; it was simply never scheduled. Recommended as
  follow-up work, consistent with this phase's own "no code, output only"
  scope.

Per this phase's "Known risks" note in the phased plan — "if any earlier
phase was skipped or partially done, this phase is where that becomes
visible, by design" — item 35 is exactly that: a real gap this audit was
built to surface, now visible and documented rather than silently absent.
No other item was found to have been claimed-done-but-actually-isn't; the
PARTIAL/BLOCKED items above were already known and documented at the time
their respective phases closed, not newly discovered gaps in previously
"complete" work.
